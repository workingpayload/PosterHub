## Module Map

### di/ServiceLocator.kt [config]
- **Does**: App-wide manual DI singleton. Builds OkHttpClient (DNS-over-HTTPS via Cloudflare, to dodge ISP DNS blocks on tmdb hosts), two Retrofit clients (TMDB, fanart.tv), repos, DataStore-backed stores.
- **Exports**: `ServiceLocator.init()`, `.movieRepository`, `.posterRepository`, `.favoritesStore`, `.settingsStore`, `.okHttpClient`
- **Depends on**: `data.remote.*`, `data.repository.*`, `data.local.*`, `BuildConfig` (TMDB_API_KEY, FANART_API_KEY)

### data/repository/MovieRepository.kt [service]
- **Does**: Movie/TV feeds, search, details+cast+similar, backed by `TmdbApi`.
- **Depends on**: `data.remote.TmdbApi`, `data.remote.dto.Dtos`, `domain.model.Models`

### data/repository/PosterRepository.kt [service]
- **Does**: Combines TMDB + fanart.tv poster candidates into `PosterImage` list (dedup/rank by resolution).
- **Depends on**: `data.remote.TmdbApi`, `data.remote.FanartApi`, `domain.model.Models`

### domain/model/Models.kt [model]
- **Exports**: `Movie`, `CastMember`, `PosterImage`, `MediaType`, `ImageSource`
- **Notes**: `PosterImage.isUltraHd` = width>=1800 (core "4K poster" business rule)

### ui/nav/Routes.kt + PosterNav.kt [route]
- **Does**: Type-safe (`@Serializable`) nav routes (Home/Search/Favorites/About/Detail/Fullscreen) + `PosterApp()` NavHost with shared-element transitions between poster thumb → detail hero → fullscreen.
- **Depends on**: all `ui.*` screens

### util/ViewModelFactory.kt [util]
- **Does**: Builds ViewModels from `ServiceLocator` singletons (no Hilt/Dagger).

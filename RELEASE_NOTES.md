# PostersHub v2.0.0

Discover, download, favorite, and set high-quality official posters directly from your Android device — **completely ad-free**.

## 🎨 About PostersHub

**PostersHub** makes it easy to discover and save high-quality official posters in one place. Find artwork you love, mark it as a favorite, download it to your device, or set it directly as your wallpaper.

No annoying ads. No distractions. Just posters.

## ✨ Features

* 🖼️ Browse high-quality official posters
* 🔍 Easily discover posters
* ❤️ Mark posters as **Favorites** for quick access
* ⬇️ Download posters directly to your device
* 🖼️ **Set posters directly as your wallpaper**
* 📱 Simple and clean Android experience
* 🚫 **100% ad-free**
* 🎨 Enjoy high-quality artwork on your device

## 🧩 Also in This Release

* 🔎 **Search filters & recent searches** — narrow results to Movies or TV, with your last searches suggested instantly
* 🎬 **Richer detail pages** — genres, runtime, full cast, and a "More Like This" row of similar titles
* 🏷️ **Resolution & source badges** — see at a glance which posters are 4K, and whether they came from TMDB or Fanart, textless or not
* 📶 **Offline-ready Favorites** — saved posters are cached on-device and still render with no connection
* 🔃 **Pull-to-refresh**, **swipe-to-delete** on Favorites, and sort Favorites by Recently Added / Title / Top Rated
* 🌗 **Optional system theme** — follow your device's light/dark mode and Material You colors instead of the built-in cinematic dark theme
* 📤 **Share posters** — long-press any poster for a quick Favorite/Share menu, or share straight from the detail screen
* 🔔 **Smarter error handling** — a failed section retries on its own instead of blanking the whole screen, with clear messages for "no internet" vs "bad API key"
* 📵 **Mobile data warning** — a heads-up before downloading or setting wallpaper on a metered connection
* 🎯 **Native splash screen**, **predictive back gesture**, **haptic feedback**, and **adaptive layouts** for tablets and landscape
* ⚙️ **New Settings screen** — theme toggle, clear image cache, app version, and required TMDB / fanart.tv attribution
